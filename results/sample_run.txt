Task 1 | BFS and DFS on the manual graph (A -> G)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
graph: {'A': ['B', 'C'], 'B': ['D', 'E'], 'C': ['F'], 'D': [], 'E': ['G'], 'F': [], 'G': []}

DFS step-by-step  (frontier = stack, top = last)
#  | node | path    | added  | frontier
1  | A    | A       | C,B    | ['AC', 'AB']
2  | B    | AB      | E,D    | ['AC', 'ABE', 'ABD']
3  | D    | ABD     | -      | ['AC', 'ABE']
4  | E    | ABE     | G      | ['AC', 'ABEG']
5  | G    | ABEG    | GOAL   | ['AC']
DFS expansion order: A B D E G
DFS path: A -> B -> E -> G

BFS step-by-step  (frontier = queue, front = first)
#  | node | path    | added  | frontier
1  | A    | A       | B,C    | ['AB', 'AC']
2  | B    | AB      | D,E    | ['AC', 'ABD', 'ABE']
3  | C    | AC      | F      | ['ABD', 'ABE', 'ACF']
4  | D    | ABD     | -      | ['ABE', 'ACF']
5  | E    | ABE     | G      | ['ACF', 'ABEG']
6  | F    | ACF     | -      | ['ABEG']
7  | G    | ABEG    | GOAL   | []
BFS expansion order: A B C D E F G
BFS path: A -> B -> E -> G

Cross-check with the manual functions:
  bfs('A','G') = ['A', 'B', 'E', 'G']  same as trace: True
  dfs('A','G') = ['A', 'B', 'E', 'G']  same as trace: True
  BFS expanded 7 nodes, DFS only 5 - but BFS guarantees the fewest edges.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
DLS from A to G:
limit | result  | path       | #expanded | expanded nodes
4     | FOUND   | A->B->E->G | 5         | A B D E G
3     | FOUND   | A->B->E->G | 5         | A B D E G
2     | CUTOFF  | none       | 6         | A B D E C F
1     | CUTOFF  | none       | 3         | A B C

IDS from A to G with different maximum limits:
max | result  | path       | rounds (limit:result:#nodes)      | total expanded
1   | CUTOFF  | none       | 0:CUTOFF:1 1:CUTOFF:3             | 4
2   | CUTOFF  | none       | 0:CUTOFF:1 1:CUTOFF:3 2:CUTOFF:6  | 10
3   | FOUND   | A->B->E->G | 0:CUTOFF:1 1:CUTOFF:3 2:CUTOFF:6 3:FOUND:5 | 15
4   | FOUND   | A->B->E->G | 0:CUTOFF:1 1:CUTOFF:3 2:CUTOFF:6 3:FOUND:5 | 15

Repeated work inside IDS (max limit 3):
  round 0: A  [CUTOFF]
  round 1: A B C  [CUTOFF]
  round 2: A B D E C F  [CUTOFF]
  round 3: A B D E G  [FOUND]

Edge cases:
  goal not reachable (D->G, limit 5): FAILURE, expanded ['D']
  start is the goal (A->A, limit 0): FOUND A
  graph with cycles (F->A, E->B): {'A': ['B', 'C'], 'B': ['D', 'E'], 'C': ['F'], 'D': [], 'E': ['B', 'G'], 'F': ['A'], 'G': []}
    limit 1: CUTOFF none, 3 nodes expanded - terminates
    limit 3: FOUND A->B->E->G, 5 nodes expanded - terminates
    limit 8: FOUND A->B->E->G, 5 nodes expanded - terminates
    missing goal X, limit 12: FAILURE - the path check stops the loops

Task 3 | Uniform Cost Search vs BFS (weighted graph)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Edges:
  A->B = 1
  A->C = 2
  B->D = 5
  B->E = 1
  C->F = 2
  C->G = 10  <- new corridor
  E->G = 1

UCS log:
# | node | g  | path | added (node=g) | frontier after
1 | A    | 0  | A    | B=1 C=2        | {AB:1, AC:2}
2 | B    | 1  | AB   | D=6 E=2        | {AC:2, ABE:2, ABD:6}
3 | C    | 2  | AC   | F=4 G=12       | {ABE:2, ACF:4, ABD:6, ACG:12}
4 | E    | 2  | ABE  | G=3            | {ABEG:3, ACF:4, ABD:6, ACG:12}
5 | G    | 3  | ABEG | GOAL           | {ACF:4, ABD:6, ACG:12}
UCS expansion order: A B C E G
UCS path A-B-E-G, cost 3; check: AB(1) + BE(1) + EG(1) = 3 -> correct

BFS expansion order: A B C D E F G
BFS path A-C-G, 2 edges, cost AC(2) + CG(10) = 12

Comparison:
method | path    | edges | cost
UCS    | A-B-E-G | 3     | 3
BFS    | A-C-G   | 2     | 12
-> BFS finds the path with fewer edges, UCS finds the cheaper one.

Edge cases:
  unreachable (F->G): cost=None, path=None
  start is goal (C->C): cost=0, path=['C']
  with cycles (F->A, E->B): cost=3, path=A-B-E-G, expanded A B C E G
